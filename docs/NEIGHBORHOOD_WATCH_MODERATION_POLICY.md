# Neighborhood Watch — Moderation Policy

NW is safety-focused, not an open social network.

Users may report posts/comments for: spam, harassment, threat, false information, privacy violation, unrelated content, dangerous content, other.

Authorized moderators may: review, hide, restore, remove, warn, suspend posting privileges (where membership moderation allows). Soft-delete preferred for audit.

Do not silently auto-delete based only on an unreviewed classifier. Cross-community moderation is denied.
